Radiant InfoTech Nepal Pvt. Ltd. established in 2003, has been delivering end-to-end technology solutions for over 22 years — trusted by small and medium businesses, institutions, banks, hospitals, insurance companies, NGOs, and government agencies across Nepal.

Our background is rooted in web and software development, providing a full range of web, desktop, and digital signature solutions. We believe information technology breaks the traditional boundaries of time and space — enabling businesses to grow commercially, socially, and economically. With expertise in ASP, Java, PHP, MSSQL, MySQL, Oracle, and PKI technology, we serve every client with the same standard: excellence.

Our Journey

From a small team of innovators to a trusted technology partner in Nepal since 2003

How We Started

Radiant Infotech Nepal was established in 2003 as a boutique digital agency committed to providing quality digital services. Starting with web application development and software solutions, the company has grown into a full-service technology provider serving individuals, firms, schools, banks, hospitals, insurance companies, NGOs, and government sectors across Nepal and beyond.

Where We're Going

Today, we stand at the forefront of digital transformation in Nepal — helping businesses with digital signature certificates, PKI technology, web solutions, software development, graphic design, and ICT consulting. We envision a future where technology seamlessly integrates with every business process across Nepal.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

We measure our success by your satisfaction. Our goal is to help people, businesses, organizations, and governments develop solid, functional, and beneficial information-sharing systems through information technology — enabling commercial, social, and economic growth. We work with our clients for the long term to help achieve their goals through quality and consistent value.
