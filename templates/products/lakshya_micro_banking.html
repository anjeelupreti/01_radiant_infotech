Key Features

Complete Feature Set

  • Loan Management — Supports loans, savings, and deposits with full lifecycle management
  • Automated Processing — Automated loan disbursement, collection, and repayment tracking
  • Real-Time Reporting — Real-time reporting and analytics dashboard
  • Customizable Workflows — Customizable workflow management for loan processing
  • Customer Management — Integrated customer management system with 360° view
  • Branch Support — Supports branch-level operations with multi-user access
  • Role-Based Permissions — Granular role-based access control for security
  • Regulatory Compliance — Handles regulatory compliance and reporting requirements
  • Third-Party Integration — Seamless integration with mobile wallets and payment gateways
  • Cloud Access — Secure, cloud-based access for remote management

Loan Management Capabilities

  • Loan Types — Individual loans, group loans, emergency loans, agricultural loans, and more
  • Loan Application Processing — Digital loan application with document management
  • Credit Scoring — Automated credit scoring based on customer history
  • Disbursement Management — Automated loan disbursement with multiple payment methods
  • Repayment Scheduling — Flexible repayment schedules (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly)
  • Collection Management — Automated collection tracking and overdue notifications
  • Interest Calculation — Support for flat rate, declining balance, and compound interest
  • Loan Restructuring — Handle loan rescheduling and restructuring requests
  • Write-Off Management — Manage loan write-offs and provisions
  • Guarantor Management — Track loan guarantors and their obligations

Reports & Analytics

  • Portfolio Reports — Loan portfolio quality, aging analysis, and risk classification
  • Financial Statements — Balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow reports
  • Collection Reports — Daily collection reports and collection efficiency tracking
  • Savings Reports — Savings portfolio reports and interest calculations
  • Regulatory Reports — Central Bank and regulatory compliance reports
  • Branch Performance — Branch-wise performance reports and comparisons
  • Customer Reports — Customer portfolio reports and transaction history
  • Audit Trail Reports — Complete audit logs for compliance
  • Dashboard Analytics — Real-time KPI dashboard with charts and graphs
  • Export Options — Export reports to PDF, Excel, and CSV formats

Use Cases

  • Microfinance Institutions — Complete microfinance operations management
  • Cooperatives — Member management, savings, and loan processing for cooperatives
  • Community Banks — Community banking operations and customer management
  • Self-Help Groups — Group loan management for SHGs and JLGs
  • Agricultural Finance — Specialized loan products for farmers and agricultural businesses
  • Women's Empowerment Programs — Micro-loan programs for women entrepreneurs
  • Small Business Lending — Small business loan origination and management
  • Education Finance — Education loan management for students and parents
