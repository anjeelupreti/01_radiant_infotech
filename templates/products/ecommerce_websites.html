  • Unlimited Products — Add and manage unlimited products in your catalog

  • Order Processing — Complete order lifecycle management from cart to delivery
  • Order Status — Real-time order status tracking (pending, confirmed, shipped, delivered)

  • Order Export — Export orders to CSV/Excel for accounting
  • Multi-currency Support — Display prices and process payments in multiple currencies
