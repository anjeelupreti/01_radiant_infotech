What We Design

Our Design Services

We offer a full range of graphic design services for businesses at every stage:

  • Logo & Brand Identity — Original logo design with full brand identity packages including guidelines, colour systems, and typography
  • UI/UX Design — Website and mobile app interface design — wireframes, prototypes, and final visual designs
  • Print Design — Business cards, letterheads, brochures, flyers, posters, banners, and packaging
  • Social Media Design — Post templates, story graphics, cover images, and ad creatives for all major platforms
  • Presentation Design — Professional PowerPoint and Keynote presentation decks for pitches, reports, and events
  • Illustration & Infographics — Custom illustrations, icons, and data-driven infographics for reports and content marketing

Tools & Software We Work With

We use industry-standard tools to ensure professional quality and easy file handoff:

  • Adobe Illustrator — Vector logo design, icons, and print-ready artwork
  • Adobe Photoshop — Photo manipulation, digital art, and raster graphics
  • Adobe InDesign — Multi-page layouts, brochures, magazines, and reports
  • Figma — UI/UX design, prototyping, and design system creation
  • Adobe XD — Interactive app and website prototyping
  • Canva Pro — Social media templates and quick-turnaround marketing assets
  • Deliverables: Print-ready PDFs, web-optimised PNGs/JPGs, SVG vectors, and Figma source files

Our Design Process

A collaborative process that puts your vision at the centre of every project:

  • Step 1 — Brief & Discovery: We understand your brand, audience, goals, and preferences through a detailed design brief
  • Step 2 — Research & Concepts: We research your industry and competitors, then develop initial design concepts for your review
  • Step 3 — First Presentation: 2–3 distinct design directions presented with rationale so you can choose a direction
  • Step 4 — Refinement: We refine the chosen direction based on your feedback through revision rounds
  • Step 5 — Final Delivery: All final files delivered in agreed formats — print-ready, web-ready, and source files included
