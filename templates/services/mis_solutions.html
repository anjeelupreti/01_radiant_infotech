Our MIS Solutions

Hospitality & Travel Management Systems

Comprehensive solutions for hotels, travel agencies, and tourism businesses.

  • Hotel Management System — Complete hotel operations including reservation management, check-in/check-out, room allocation, housekeeping integration, billing, and guest history tracking
  • Travel Agent Management System — Tour package management, booking systems, customer records, itinerary planning, and vendor management
  • CMS for Tours and Travels — Complete control over tour packages, reservations, and customer records with multi-location support and role-based permissions

Key Features:

  • Automated booking confirmations and payment handling
  • Live reporting and sales performance tracking
  • Integrated CRM for client preferences and travel history
  • Multi-location operations with user-specific roles
  • Integration with payment systems and travel APIs
  • Cloud-based secure platform for remote management

Healthcare Management Systems

Complete hospital and healthcare facility management solutions.

  • Hospital Management System — Comprehensive healthcare facility management including patient registration, appointment scheduling, OPD/IPD management, pharmacy, billing, laboratory, radiology, EMR, and reporting

Education Management Systems

School and college management solutions for academic institutions.

  • School and College Management System — Student registration, attendance tracking, examination management, fee collection, timetable, teacher management, library, transport, and academic reporting
  • Finch School Management System — Specialized school management software developed by Radiant Infotech

Finance & Cooperative Management Systems

Solutions for financial institutions and cooperatives.

  • Cooperative and Financial Institutions Management System — Member registration, savings accounts, loan processing, share capital, dividend calculation, transaction management, and financial reporting
  • Lakshya Micro Banking Software — Specialized microfinance and micro-banking software with comprehensive management of microfinance operations and customer accounts

Human Resource & Payroll Management Systems

Complete workforce management solutions.

  • Human Resource Attendance and Payroll Management System — Employee records, attendance tracking, leave management, payroll processing, performance appraisal, recruitment, and HR reporting
  • WorkForce Mini Accounting — Specialized workforce and accounting solution developed by Radiant Infotech

Inventory, Accounting & Supply Chain Systems

Complete business operations management solutions.

  • Inventory and Fixed Assets Management System — Stock tracking, asset lifecycle management, procurement, warehouse management, and fixed asset depreciation
  • Accounting System Management System — General ledger, accounts payable/receivable, bank reconciliation, trial balance, financial statements, and tax computation
  • Product and Chain Management System — Product catalog, supply chain tracking, distribution network management, and order processing
  • Dealers and Distributions Management System — Dealer network management, commission tracking, sales order processing, and distribution analytics
