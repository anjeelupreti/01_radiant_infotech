What We Offer

Our Data Entry Services

We handle a wide range of data entry and data processing tasks:

  • Document Digitisation — Convert paper records, handwritten documents, and scanned files into structured digital formats
  • Form & Survey Processing — Extract and organise data from physical or digital forms, questionnaires, and surveys
  • Database Entry & Management — Populate, update, and maintain databases with clean, structured data
  • Data Cleansing & Deduplication — Identify and remove errors, duplicates, and inconsistencies from existing datasets
  • Product Data Entry — E-commerce product listings including descriptions, pricing, images, and category mapping
  • Invoice & Financial Data Entry — Accurate entry of invoices, receipts, purchase orders, and financial records

Formats & Tools We Work With

We accept data in any form and deliver in your preferred output format:

  • Input Formats: Paper documents, scanned PDFs, images (JPG/PNG), Word documents, Excel files, CSV, XML, JSON
  • Output Formats: Excel / CSV, Google Sheets, SQL database, XML, JSON, PDF, or any custom format you specify
  • Spreadsheet Tools: Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice Calc
  • Database Tools: MySQL, PostgreSQL, Microsoft Access, Airtable
  • OCR & Scanning: Adobe Acrobat, ABBYY FineReader for document recognition and conversion
  • Collaboration: Secure file transfer via email, Google Drive, Dropbox, or SFTP based on your preference

Our Data Entry Process

A reliable, quality-controlled process from intake to final delivery:

  • Step 1 — Requirement Review: We review your source data, volume, output requirements, and deadline to provide an accurate quote
  • Step 2 — NDA & Confidentiality: Non-disclosure agreement signed before any data is shared with our team
  • Step 3 — Sample Batch: A small sample batch is completed first so you can verify accuracy and format before full processing begins
  • Step 4 — Data Entry & QC: Data entered by our team and independently verified through a double-entry quality control check
  • Step 5 — Delivery: Final dataset delivered in your required format within the agreed deadline
  • Step 6 — Review & Corrections: Any corrections or adjustments handled promptly at no extra charge
