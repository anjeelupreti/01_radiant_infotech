What We Offer

Available Certificate Classes

We provide all major DSC classes to suit your specific compliance needs:

  • Class 3 Individual DSC — For professionals, freelancers, and individuals needing strong identity authentication
  • Class 3 Organisation DSC — For companies filing returns, participating in tenders, or signing contracts
  • Document Signer Certificate — For automated, high-volume PDF/document signing workflows
  • SSL/TLS Certificates — Secure your web applications and APIs with trusted certificates
  • Code Signing Certificates — Authenticate software distributions and prevent tampering
  • Encrypted Email Certificates (S/MIME) — Digitally sign and encrypt email communications

Where Digital Signatures Are Used

Digital signatures are now essential across nearly every sector in Nepal:

  • E-tendering and government procurement portals
  • MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs) ROC filings
  • Income tax e-filing and audit reports
  • Banking — loan agreements, account opening, KYC
  • Legal documents — MoUs, contracts, NDAs
  • HR — appointment letters, payroll, policy acknowledgements
  • Healthcare — prescription records, patient consent forms
  • Real estate — property sale deeds and registrations
