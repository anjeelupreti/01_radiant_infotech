Certificate Classes

Class 1 Certificates

Personal email certificates that allow you to secure your email messages. These certificates are ideal for individual users who need basic digital signature functionality.

  • Digitally Sign Email — Assure recipients that the email has come from you with digital signature
  • Encrypt Email — Prevent unauthorized people from reading your email communications
  • Authenticate to Web Servers — Engage in secure communication with web servers, protecting sensitive information like credit card details

Class 2 Certificates

Managed Digital Certificates issued to employees, partners, affiliates, and customers of business and government organizations. The organization verifies the accuracy of information submitted.

  • Organizational Verification — Verification done by the organization functioning as a Registration Authority (RA)
  • Sub-CA Setup Available — Organizations can have Sub-CA setup under the CA trust network
  • Employee/Partner Certificates — Ideal for organizations issuing certificates to their staff and affiliates

Class 3 Certificates

Highest level of trust, issued to individuals, companies, and government organizations. Requires physical presence before a Registration Authority for identity verification.

  • Electronic Commerce Applications — e-banking, e-commerce, e-governance, and financial transactions
  • Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) — Secure business-to-business document exchange
  • Membership-Based Online Services — Premium access services requiring strong authentication
  • Physical Verification Required — Applicant must be physically present before a Registration Authority
