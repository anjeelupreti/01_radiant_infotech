What We Offer

Our Mobile Development Services

We cover every type of mobile application your business may need:

  • Android App Development — Native Android apps built with Kotlin or Java for the full Google ecosystem
  • iOS App Development — Native iPhone and iPad apps built with Swift for seamless Apple platform integration
  • Cross-Platform Apps — Single codebase for both platforms using Flutter or React Native, reducing cost and time to market
  • Business & Enterprise Apps — Internal tools, field apps, and employee-facing applications for operational efficiency
  • E-Commerce Mobile Apps — Fully featured shopping apps with cart, payments, and order tracking
  • App Maintenance & Upgrades — Updates, OS compatibility fixes, feature additions, and performance improvements for existing apps

Technologies We Work With

We use proven, widely supported technologies across all our mobile projects:

  • Cross-Platform: Flutter (Dart), React Native (JavaScript)
  • Native Android: Kotlin, Java — Android Studio
  • Native iOS: Swift, Objective-C — Xcode
  • Backend / API: Django REST Framework, Node.js, Firebase
  • Database: PostgreSQL, MySQL, Firebase Realtime DB, SQLite
  • Cloud & Push: AWS, Google Cloud, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), APNs
  • App Stores: Google Play Store and Apple App Store submission and management

Our App Development Process

A clear, structured process that keeps you informed and in control at every stage:

  • Step 1 — Discovery & Planning: Define features, user flows, platform targets, and project scope together
  • Step 2 — UI/UX Design: Wireframes and interactive prototypes reviewed and approved before development begins
  • Step 3 — Development: Sprint-based development with regular builds shared for your testing and feedback
  • Step 4 — QA & Testing: Device testing, performance profiling, and bug fixes across real Android and iOS devices
  • Step 5 — Store Submission: We handle the full Google Play and App Store submission process
  • Step 6 — Post-Launch Support: Monitoring, crash reporting, and maintenance packages available
