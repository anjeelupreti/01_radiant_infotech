Our Digital Marketing Services

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

From documentation: Search engine optimization is a methodology of strategies, techniques and tactics used to increase the amount of visitors to a website by obtaining a high-ranking placement in the search results page of a search engine (SERP) — including Google, Bing, Yahoo and other search engines.

  • White Hat SEO Practices — Ethical, sustainable SEO techniques that follow search engine guidelines for long-term results
  • Keyword Research & Strategy — Identifying high-value keywords that your target audience uses to find businesses like yours
  • On-Page Optimization — Meta tags, header optimization, content structuring, internal linking, and image optimization
  • Off-Page Optimization — Quality backlink building, directory submissions, and local SEO for Nepal-based businesses
  • Technical SEO — Site speed optimization, mobile-friendliness, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, and structured data markup
  • Local SEO — Google Business Profile optimization, local citations, and location-based targeting for Nepali businesses
  • SEO Audits & Reporting — Comprehensive website audits and monthly performance reports with actionable insights

Why SEO Matters: It is common practice for Internet users to not click through pages and pages of search results. The higher a website naturally ranks in organic results, the greater the chance that site will be visited by a user.

Email Marketing Services

From documentation: Email marketing is directly marketing a commercial message to a group of people using email. It usually involves using email to send ads, request business, or solicit sales, and is meant to build loyalty, trust, or brand awareness.

  • Mass Mailing System — Send emails to any contact group at a time using our powerful mass mailing platform
  • Email Campaign Management — End-to-end campaign planning, execution, and performance tracking
  • Newsletter Marketing — Regular newsletters to keep your audience engaged and informed about your business
  • Promotional Email Campaigns — Targeted email blasts for product launches, special offers, and events
  • Automated Email Sequences — Drip campaigns for lead nurturing, welcome series, and follow-ups
  • Email List Management — Segmentation, list cleaning, and subscriber management for optimal deliverability
  • Performance Analytics — Open rates, click-through rates, conversion tracking, and A/B testing

Email Marketing Benefits: Build loyalty, trust, and brand awareness while directly reaching potential and current customers with your message.

Facebook Advertising Services

From documentation: Adverts on Facebook are unique. They're shown to specific groups of highly engaged people on desktop and mobile. When your adverts have great creative content and are well targeted, they get more likes, comments and shares.

  • Targeted Facebook Ads — Reach specific audiences based on demographics, interests, behaviors, and location
  • Boosted Page Posts — Expand your page's reach by boosting high-performing posts to a wider audience
  • Audience Expansion — When someone engages with your ad, their friends may see it, making your advertising more powerful
  • Campaign Objectives — Choose from awareness, consideration, or conversion goals based on your business needs
  • Ad Creative Development — Professional ad design, copywriting, and video creation for maximum engagement
  • Retargeting Campaigns — Re-engage visitors who have interacted with your website or previous ads
  • Performance Tracking — Detailed analytics and reporting on reach, engagement, leads, and sales

Facebook Advertising Goals: Connect with more people through your Page, find the right customers for your app, or drive sales on your website or in your shop.
